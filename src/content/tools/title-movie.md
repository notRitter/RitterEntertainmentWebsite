## Set the tone before the game starts

Play video before the title screen or after it goes idle. Combine foreground and background video layers with playback, opacity, and transition controls.

## Design the menu during playtest

Use presets for an animated title menu, add custom commands and interactive widgets, and adjust the layout in the live developer editor during NW.js playtest. The storefront includes setup details and a trailer.
