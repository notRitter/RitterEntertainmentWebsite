## Play with numbers at speed

Practice addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division in a side-scrolling arcade game. Solve the problem as you move and steer your character into the right answer. There are six difficulty levels, from Very Easy through Insanity.

## Challenge Mode

Choose a subject and a difficulty, then clear its stages without collecting an incorrect answer. Completing a subject at a given difficulty unlocks a character. The unlock guide lists the rewards by subject and difficulty.

## Freestyle Mode

Play for a high score with a limited number of lives. Choose one subject or try Mixed to get problems from all four.

The browser release is free or pay what you want on itch.io. Game backgrounds include work by [Digital Moons](https://digitalmoons.itch.io/).
