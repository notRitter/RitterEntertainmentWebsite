## Take a character into the world

Explore the open world, form a party, and venture into instanced dungeons. In combat, read enemy attack patterns and dodge projectiles while working through encounters and bosses.

## Build around what you find

Deathwake uses persistent accounts and characters. Multiple classes, character attributes, and equipment give players ways to shape a build. Loot has rarity tiers and procedural properties, and account-wide storage lets players set items aside for other characters.

## Permanent stakes

Character death is permanent, and fallen characters leave corpses that can be looted. Parties allow cooperative play, while PvP hostility adds another risk to encounters with other players.

## Multiplayer technology

Deathwake uses a Phaser client and an authoritative Colyseus server. The server governs shared game state and multiplayer rules.
