## Build and defend a base

Hand of God is a god-themed strategy game in development. Its systems center on developing a base, managing resources, and building defenses that can withstand enemy raids.

## Command divine units

Powerful divine units are part of the defense. Use them alongside the base’s defensive systems as enemy forces raid your territory.
