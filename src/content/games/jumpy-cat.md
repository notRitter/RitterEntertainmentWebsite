## Two tiles at a time

Every hop covers two tiles. Read the traffic, time your jumps onto drifting lily pads, and try to beat your best distance. Your best run is saved in the browser you play in.

## Controls

On desktop, use the arrow keys or WASD to hop and Enter or R to retry. On touch screens, swipe in the direction you want to jump. You can also enable a directional pad in Options.

## Built with Ritter tools

This RPG Maker MZ game is a playable example of the Ultimate Event Spawner and Boundary System. The two tools work together to manage traffic, lily pads, and events around Jumpy as the game moves along.
